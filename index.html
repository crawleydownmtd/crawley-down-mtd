About Crawley Down MTD

Crawley Down MTD is a local bookkeeping practice focused on helping sole traders and small businesses manage their bookkeeping, stay organised and prepare for Making Tax Digital.

I understand that most business owners would rather spend their time earning money than worrying about paperwork, receipts and HMRC deadlines.

Using modern cloud bookkeeping software and digital workflows, I help clients maintain organised digital records without needing to deliver paperwork or visit a high street office.

Every business is different, so I recommend a suitable, cost-effective software solution based on its needs and budget, including Xero or FreeAgent where appropriate.

The service is based in Crawley Down and supports clients in East Grinstead, Copthorne, Turners Hill and surrounding parts of West Sussex.

Bookkeeping Support at the Level You Need

Whether you want to keep control of your own bookkeeping, hand the routine work over, or need help with a one-off project, there are three straightforward ways to work with Crawley Down MTD.

OPTION 1

Bookkeeping Help & Guidance

£60 per hour

For businesses that want to maintain their own records but need professional guidance. Available remotely or through an agreed local face-to-face appointment.

One-hour minimum for face-to-face appointments.

OPTION 2

Managed Bookkeeping

From £75 per month

For businesses that would rather have the routine bookkeeping handled for them. The final fee depends on the volume and complexity of the agreed work.

OPTION 3

Bespoke / Project Support

Quoted individually

For setup, catch-up, tidy-up, CIS support, several accounts or entities, or another non-standard requirement. A written scope and quotation are agreed before work starts.

Not sure which option fits? Start with Bookkeeping Help & Guidance. If more work is needed, the next step and cost will be agreed before anything else is done.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you only work with Xero?

No. I consider the needs and budget of each business and can support suitable cloud solutions, including Xero and FreeAgent.

Can you help if I am not confident with bookkeeping software?

Yes. The service includes practical setup and onboarding support so that the digital process is clear from the start.

Do I have to sign up for monthly bookkeeping?

No. If you prefer to maintain your own records, Bookkeeping Help & Guidance is available at £60 per hour. Managed bookkeeping is available from £75 per month if you would rather hand the routine work over.

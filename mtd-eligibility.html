Before You Check The Examples

Making Tax Digital for Income Tax is being introduced in stages. It applies to many sole traders and landlords whose qualifying income exceeds HMRC's thresholds.

Important: The thresholds are based on qualifying gross income before expenses, not business profit. If you have both self-employment and property income, these amounts are generally considered together.

Why Start Preparing Now?

Many sole traders and landlords currently keep records using spreadsheets, paper records or a collection of receipts.

Preparing early allows you to:

  • Move to digital record keeping gradually.
  • Learn bookkeeping software before deadlines arrive.
  • Reduce stress when quarterly reporting begins.
  • Avoid a last-minute rush to become compliant.
  • Gain better visibility of your business finances.
